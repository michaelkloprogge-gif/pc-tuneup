Careful by design

A tune-up tool should never make things worse.

  • ✓Every change is listed on the History page, and almost all of them can be undone with one click.
  • ✓It asks first and shows exactly what it will do. Nothing big is pre-checked.
  • ✓A simple view for people who don't want the details: one calm screen, a status in plain words, three big buttons and only safe fixes - and a button to send a report to the family member who usually helps. One click on Advanced view shows every tool.
  • ✓Security software, VPNs, drivers and databases are never touched, and you can add your own programs to a keep list.
  • ✓As administrator it creates a Windows restore point before larger changes.
  • ✓Optional weekly maintenance cleans junk by itself and only notifies you when something new turns up.
  • ✓When Fix my PC finishes, it shows what changed - free space, apps that start with Windows, the score - before and after.
  • ✓Reminders for things that have waited too long - a restart Windows is waiting for, updates left lying, a clean-up long ago - each with a Later button.
  • ✓Every release is tested automatically on a clean Windows machine, in all seven languages.

Install

  1. Download PC-TuneUp.exe and run it. It offers to install itself for you - no administrator rights needed - and keeps itself up to date.
  2. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the app is not code-signed yet. Click More info > Run anyway.
  3. It opens in the simple view: click Make my PC faster, or fix the items in the list one by one. For every tool, click Advanced view and start with Fix my PC on the Health page. Use Restart as admin to include Windows' own files and settings.

Privacy

Everything runs on your PC. PC Tune-Up has no account, no tracking and no analytics, and nothing about your PC is sent anywhere. The only internet use is the update check (GitHub), the optional speed test (Cloudflare) and winget's program list.
